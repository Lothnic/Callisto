# Orbital Precision

A still centre and a moving satellite share one path. The mark communicates
connection through geometry: a compact core, a tilted orbit and a single point
of departure. Their spacing is deliberately calibrated rather than decorated.

Blue carries the centre; warm white carries movement. A deep ink field gives
both colours a stable ground on light and dark browser chrome. Flat colour and
clean edges keep the mark legible without lighting effects or texture.

The orbit passes behind the core and returns in front. Meticulous alignment
makes that relationship readable at a glance. Small-size versions strengthen
the path so the same silhouette survives at toolbar scale.

Quiet space is part of the form. No letters, labels or extra symbols enter the
icon. The final refinements belong to curve continuity, optical weight and
pixel coverage; craftsmanship comes from keeping those few decisions exact.
